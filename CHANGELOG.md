# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- 35 earthquakes from nine tectonic settings, recorded at up to three distances (80 recordings).
- Simple view: rotating globe, Near / Regional / Far, seismogram, six knobs, meters and output gain.
- Advanced view: world map, three-component seismogram, impulse response spectrogram, coda
  fingerprint and every parameter.
- 28 factory presets in seven categories (Vocals, Drums, Guitar & Keys, Synths & Pads,
  Mix & Ambience, Sound Design, Earth Tour), plus your own presets, favourites, search and import.
- A/B compare, undo and redo, bypass, output gain, input and output meters.
- Dark, light and match-system themes; window sizes from 75 to 200 %; three text sizes.
- Accessibility: high contrast, colour-blind palettes (red-green, blue-yellow, monochrome),
  reduced motion, screen reader support (VoiceOver, Narrator, NVDA) with spoken values,
  full keyboard control and visible focus.
- Help on hover for every control, and a "How it works" page.
- VST3, AU and Standalone for macOS (universal); VST3 and Standalone for Windows (x64).
