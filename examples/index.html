AFTERSHOCK

Sound examples for Aftershock, a reverb made from real earthquake recordings. Each one is a dry sound run through a factory preset with the real plugin. Switch between Dry and Aftershock while it plays. Dry and processed versions are matched in loudness.

Same snare, six earthquakes

One snare hit through the neutral Earth Tour settings, only the earthquake changes. Volcanic rock (Kilauea, Etna) swallows energy fast; old craton (Botswana) and far stations ring longer and darker.

Same voice, three distances

The 2020 Petrinja earthquake, recorded 179, 917 and 3,928 km away. The further the waves travel, the more highs the Earth absorbs.
